## Penalty claims require context

- **Fact:** FTC guidance lists up to **$53,088 per violation** for COPPA and CAN-SPAM. This is not an automatic fee for every child/account/message. COPPA penalty assessment is case-specific. The FTC's September 2026 notice retained 2025 levels. [COPPA penalties](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions), [CAN-SPAM](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business), [2026 notice](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-09-15/pdf/2026-18853.pdf). The amended COPPA rule's general compliance date was April 22, 2026; use the amended rule when applicability is established. [Final rule](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-04-22/html/2025-05904.htm).
- **Fact:** the 2022 Munich judgment awarded **€100** in that case. Subsequent CJEU authority says GDPR compensation requires infringement, damage, and causation; infringement alone is insufficient. [Munich judgment](https://www.gesetze-bayern.de/Content/Document/Y-300-Z-GRURRS-B-2022-N-612), [CJEU C-300/21](https://curia.europa.eu/site/upload/docs/application/pdf/2023-05/cp230072en.pdf).
- **Fact:** the official federal order quoting California Penal Code §637.2 states the civil remedy as the greater of **$5,000 per violation or three times actual damages**, after liability is established—not simply an “up to $5K” ceiling. **Limit:** direct California LegInfo access was blocked; this is a government-hosted court quotation, not a claim that every replay implementation meets the statutory elements. [Court order, page 7](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-cand-5_16-cv-06674/pdf/USCOURTS-cand-5_16-cv-06674-1.pdf).
- **Fact:** renewal disclosures concern visual proximity to the consent request; consent, acknowledgments, cancellation and notices also matter. The §17603 gift language addresses specified goods/products sent without required affirmative consent. It does not establish an automatic refund for every SaaS disclosure defect. **Limit:** the linked older court decision is case-specific; service applicability/remedies need legal review. [Official legislative analysis, page 2](https://sjud.senate.ca.gov/media/1470), [court order](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-caed-2_16-cv-01148/pdf/USCOURTS-caed-2_16-cv-01148-0.pdf). Use the [AG's current explanation](https://oag.ca.gov/node/608083), not the analysis draft, for the final July 2025 operative requirements.
- **Fact:** agent designation currently costs **$6** and must be renewed within **three years**. **$150,000 per work** is a discretionary statutory-damages maximum where willful infringement and the applicable prerequisites are established; it is not a fine for missing an agent registration. [Agent FAQ](https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/faq.html), [17 USC §504(c)](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap5.html).
